# The Patio Bella: asset inventory

Review of the 109 files uploaded to `public/images/patio-bella/_incoming/`
(99 photos, 10 videos). Every photo was opened and inspected visually; videos
were checked from frames at 15%, 50% and 85% of their length.

Files are referred to by **#number** (alphabetical order of the current
filename). The [appendix](#appendix-number-to-current-filename) maps each
number to its exact current filename. **Nothing has been moved or renamed
yet.** The proposed paths below take effect only once approved.

## Key findings

1. **Most photos have branding baked in.** 74 of 99 photos carry a "Patio
   Bella" logo watermark, an address/phone bar, or headline text. Only 25 are
   clean photographs. Small corner logos can often be cropped out; address
   bars and headline text cannot be used as photography.
2. **Resolution is Instagram-sized.** The largest photo is 1279 px wide, and
   most are 1080–1170 px. Images #1–#12 are only 730–914 px wide. These work
   well in editorial layouts (half-width or framed images), but a full-bleed
   desktop hero will look soft. If the photographer's original files exist,
   they would noticeably improve the hero and any wide images.
3. **There are two distinct venues.**
   - **Arena Mall, Nsambya:** an upper-floor dining room with a hanging-foliage
     ceiling, rattan pendant lights, green velvet chairs, a backlit bar and
     city views (#9–#12, #56, #70, #71). It also has darker, warmer rooms with
     terracotta walls and African portrait art (#23–#27, #84–#86).
   - **Lugogo Mall:** bright butter-yellow walls, painted leaf murals, cream
     chairs, glass walls onto palms (#61–#65; the cheesecake in #28 and #29
     also appears to be shot there).
4. **Views and sunsets are under-represented.** The "Above the city … golden
   sunsets" post (#23–#27) contains interiors with headline text, not view
   shots. The only clean photo showing the view is #56 (city through the
   windows, daytime). There is no clean sunset photo. If the view is central
   to the brand story, a short sunset shoot is the biggest gap to fill.
5. **The visual personality is consistent.** It combines warm amber light,
   deep green, gold, terracotta and black marble. Food is shot close and glossy
   with shallow depth of field, drinks are backlit against dark bar bokeh, and
   the brand mark is a gold brush ring with a serif "Patio". This is a strong
   base for the art direction.

| **Graphic** | Promotional graphic or menu | No; content source only |

## Full inventory with proposed organisation

The proposed paths assume three additional folders: **`brand/`** for logos,
**`video/`** for clips, and **`people-and-events/`** for guests, staff and
events. People and events are a recurring theme, and `other/` would otherwise
hold most of the best lifestyle shots.
