## 2. What the photographs say

### The suggested hero, checked

`views-and-atmosphere/arena-dining-city-view.jpg` (1170×878) **is not strong
enough to lead as a full-bleed hero**, but it is the most ownable subject in
the set.

- *For it:* the hanging-foliage canopy and rattan lanterns are unique to this
  room, guests are present, and the city shows through the glass.
- *Against it:* the image is busy and heavily processed (HDR halos), a red and
  pink mall banner fills the left windows, and at 1170 px wide it turns soft
  across a 1440–2560 px screen.
- *Verdict:* use it **framed and cropped to the canopy and lanterns** (scale
  about 1.25 from the top right, which removes the banner). It belongs at
  **sunset and night** in the page's story, not at the opening.

### What the whole set says

1. **Two rooms with opposite light.** Arena Mall is canopy green (`#10230C`)
   under lantern amber (`#B0661E`), looking over the city. Lugogo is
   butter-yellow daylight (`#E8C585`, ceiling `#DA933B`) behind glass walls
   onto palms. The Lugogo photos are all daylight; the Arena photos read as
   evening.
2. **Instagram-size photography.** Nothing is wider than 1279 px. Framed
   apertures of about 40–60% of the viewport look sharp; full-bleed looks soft.
3. **Mostly 3:4 and 4:5 portrait.** That suits phones and vertical or circular
   apertures.
4. **The food and drink photography is strong and glossy.** It is close,
   shallow-focus and suited to large scroll-scrubbed moments.
5. **The brand is social and warm, not hushed.** The posts show friends,
   families, henna, saxophone, Eid tables and Valentine's.

## 3. How the direction was chosen

The process used the Impeccable design skill (`shape`, then new-work direction
selection). Seven candidate worlds were drawn from Kampala's visual culture
and ranked by resonance:

1. Golden hour across the day: the two rooms' real light, morning to night
2. The canopy: Arena's foliage ceiling and Lugogo's painted leaves
3. African print: the print apron worn in `food/pizza-being-served.jpg`
4. Kampala hand-painted shop signage
5. Kwanjula (introduction ceremony) programmes and invitation cards
6. Barkcloth (lubugo)
7. The Kampala commuter taxi: white van, blue band, route boards

The skill uses a random roll to choose which candidate is built, so that the
designer's most familiar idea doesn't win by default. **The roll assigned
#7.** It ran in degraded mode because its service is blocked by this
environment's network policy, so no outside challenger worlds were dealt.

An **independent design review** (section 8) then judged the assigned
direction. It is specific and its call-to-action discipline is excellent, but
it **pulls the restaurant towards streetwear and transit and away from its own
gold, serif and green identity**. Its taxi reference is invisible to
non-Kampala judges and can carry "cheap and crowded" connotations locally. The
brief asks for *sophisticated*, so I am not recommending it as-is.

The recommendation is **B+**: my own top-ranked direction, raised with the two
disciplines that made A strong. A remains fully available if you prefer it.

| Option | One line | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **B+ "First Coffee to Last Light"** | The page is one Patio Bella day, in the real light of its two rooms, with A's framed apertures and an always-reachable two-stop call bar | **Recommended** |
| A "The Blue Band" | The Kampala taxi's livery as the page's system: windows, route boards, the blue band | Assigned by the roll; corrected after review |
| C Category standard | Dark full-bleed photo, centred logo, serif headline, gold button | Baseline only |

## 4. Recommended: B+ "First Coffee to Last Light"

### Concept

The homepage is **one day at Patio Bella**, told in the true light of its two
rooms:

- it opens in **Lugogo's butter-yellow morning** (the new branch makes the
  first impression);
- it moves through **midday** at the table;
- **at sunset it arrives at Arena Mall**, where the lanterns come on under the
  canopy;
- it ends in **canopy-green night** with cocktails and the rituals people come
  for.

The day structure is honest to the photographs: Lugogo's are daylight and
Arena's are evening. It gives Lugogo the first impression without demoting
Arena, which gets the climax. The brand's own line, *"Where we all come
together"*, opens and closes the page.

The feeling: **warm, generous, unhurried, local**. It should read as a
confident Kampala restaurant with two very different rooms, not as a hotel
brochure or a fast-food chain.

**Kept from A (named raises):**

- **Apertures, not full-bleed.** Every photo sits in a shaped frame (a ring, an
  arch, or a rectangle with a 14px radius), which keeps the Instagram-size
  photos sharp.
- **The two-stop call bar.** The reservation action lives in working form as
  two `tel:` stops, Lugogo first, in the first viewport. It becomes a compact
  sticky bar on phones.

### Palette

Drenched strategy: each part of the day owns its whole ground colour, and the
ground shifts as you scroll. Colours are sampled from the rooms and the logo.

| Role | Colour | Source | Use | Contrast |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Butter | `#E8C585` | Lugogo wall | Morning ground | Canopy text 10.1:1 |
| Lugogo amber | `#DA933B` | Lugogo ceiling | Midday ground | Canopy text 6.5:1 |
| Lantern | `#B0661E` | Arena lanterns | Ring, day-line marker, sunset accents; **display-size text only** (white text 4.4:1) | |
| Canopy | `#10230C` | Arena canopy | Night ground, call bar, all body text on light grounds | |
| Gold | `#CFAA5A` | Logo | Buttons and marks on Canopy only | On Canopy 7.5:1; **never text on light grounds** (2.0:1) |
| Light | `#F6EBD6` | | Text on Canopy only; **never a page ground** | 14.0:1 on Canopy |

No cream page grounds, no gradients and no glows. Colour changes are flat
fields that crossfade.

### Typography

- **Display: Gloock**, a high-contrast display serif. It is chosen because it
  sits naturally next to the logo's high-contrast "Patio" wordmark without
  imitating it. Sentence case only, no italic flourishes and no letter-spaced
  serif caps.
- **Text and UI: Hanken Grotesk** (variable). Warm and very legible at small
  sizes.
- **Brand mark:** the official logo, never retyped.

Hierarchy:

| Level | Setting |
| --- | --- |
| Display XL (hero) | Gloock, `clamp(48px, 6.6vw, 104px)`, leading 0.95, tracking -0.02em |
| Section headline | Gloock, `clamp(36px, 4.8vw, 72px)` |
| Stop name | Gloock, 28px |
| Body | Hanken 400, 17–19px / 1.5, measure ≤ 62ch |
| Labels (day line, nav) | Hanken 600, ≥14px, tracked 0.08–0.12em, uppercase via CSS `text-transform` |
| Phone numbers | Hanken 600, 20px, tabular figures |

No kickers above headlines. Labels appear only where they do a job: the day
line, nav and stop metadata.

### Components

- **Header:** the logo (52px, on its black disc), four links (Lugogo · Arena
  Mall · The day · Rituals) and a **Reserve** pill. Reserve opens a
  **two-stop chooser** (Lugogo or Arena Mall, each a `tel:` link). It never
  dials a single number blindly. On scroll the header compacts and gains a
  small **day indicator** (Morning · Midday · Sunset · Night) showing where you
  are in the page.
- **Apertures:**
  - **Ring:** a circle with a lantern-coloured ring offset from the image.
    The CSS ring stands in for the logo's brush ring; a supplied vector of the
    real brush ring would replace it.
  - **Arch:** a round-topped frame taken from Lugogo's real arched doorways.
  - **Plate:** a rectangle with a 14px radius, for food and detail shots.
  - No other card types exist.
- **Call bar:** a Canopy bar split into two stops. Each half is **one `tel:`
  link** with no nested button, showing the stop name, NEW tag (Lugogo), mall
  name, number and "Call to reserve". Lugogo is always first. On phones,
  after the hero, it becomes a 56px sticky bar ("Lugogo · Arena Mall", each
  half "Call to reserve"), respecting the iOS safe area.
- **Buttons and links:** pills. The primary pill is Canopy with Butter text on
  light grounds, and Gold with Canopy text on Canopy. Hover darkens the pill
  and the label shifts 2px. Text links get an underline that draws from left
  to right. No shadows.
- **Day line:** a 2px Canopy rule with four labelled stops and a lantern
  marker. It appears in the hero and as the header indicator.

### Signature interaction: the sun ring

The logo's ring becomes the sun of the page's day:

- **Hero:** the ring draws itself around Lugogo's morning.
- **Throughout:** the day indicator in the header advances as you scroll.
- **At sunset** (section 3), the ring holds steady while its image sets. The
  saxophone slides down out of the circle as the Arena canopy rises into it,
  and the page ground darkens from Amber to Canopy.

It is one idea, used three times, and it carries the story: two rooms, one
day.

It works on phones without pinning: the colour crossfade and ring swap are
scroll-linked, and on desktop the sunset section is pinned for a short 120%
scroll. It never hijacks horizontal scroll, and focus order follows the
document.

### Animation choreography

This uses the existing `src/lib/motion` system (`docs/MOTION.md`). There is no
new library and no competing system.

**Hero intro (about 1.8 seconds, once per load; `data-intro` and
`releaseIntro()`):**

| Time | Movement | Tool |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.0s | The ring outline draws (a single SVG circle stroke, `stroke-dashoffset`) | timeline |
| 0.2s | The Lugogo image opens through a growing circle (`clip-path: circle()`), with a slight counter-scale 1.15 → 1 | `clipReveal` with a new `from: "circle"` option |
| 0.3s | Headline lines rise out of their masks | `splitReveal({ by: "lines", timeline })` |
| 0.8s | The arch slides up 24px into place | timeline `from` |
| 1.0s | The day-line rule draws left to right; the marker drops onto Morning | timeline `scaleX` / `from` |
| 1.1s | The call bar rises from below (`yPercent: 100 → 0`) | timeline `from` |

**On scroll:**

| Moment | Behaviour | Tool |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ground colour | A fixed backdrop of stacked colour layers (Butter / Amber / Canopy) crossfades by opacity as each section crosses mid-viewport. This avoids repainting large areas. | scrub tweens on layer `opacity` |
| Day indicator | The marker moves between the four stops | ScrollTrigger `onToggle` per section |
| Section images | Rings and arches open with a circle or bottom clip; plates with a bottom clip | `clipReveal` with `scrollTrigger` |
| Pizza | Scrub scale 1.25 → 1 with a small drift | `scrubMedia` |
| Sunset (desktop/tablet) | Pinned for 120%: the saxophone image `yPercent 0 → 100` out of the ring while the canopy image comes in from -100 → 0; ground layers crossfade to Canopy; headline lines reveal | `pinnedScene` |
| Sunset (phone) | The same timeline, not pinned, scrubbed over the section's own height | `responsive()` branch |
| Section headlines | Masked line reveals | `splitReveal` with `scrollTrigger` |
| Dish lists and rituals | Staggered entrances, batched | `batchReveal` |

**Hover (fine pointers only):** apertures scale their image 1.04 inside the
frame (`quickTo`), and links draw their underline.

**Flip:** used for one real layout change. The header **Reserve** pill
expands into the two-stop chooser panel (pill → panel), and the panel collapses
back on close. It is also used later on `/menu` for category filtering.

**Reduced motion:** no pin, scrub, parallax or split text, and no ring
drawing. Each section simply has its own ground colour, and images appear in
place. **Without JavaScript** everything is visible and readable; only GSAP
applies hidden states (`docs/MOTION.md`).

### Responsive behaviour

| Width | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| ≥1024 | As described; short sunset pin; header day indicator |
| 768–1023 | Hero stacks: headline on top, then ring and arch side by side; sunset pin shortened to 80%; day indicator hidden |
| <768 | No pins. The ring bleeds off the right edge; the 56px sticky call bar appears after the hero; overlapping image clusters become two-column staggers; dish lists become a single column. Tap targets are at least 48px. |

Images use `next/image` with `sizes` matched to aperture widths (ring at
about 40vw desktop, 82vw phone). The hero ring and arch use `priority`;
everything else lazy-loads. Apertures have fixed aspect ratios, so there is no
layout shift.

### Risks

- **Familiar territory.** A serif display on warm ground is a common
  restaurant look, and a common AI default. Mitigations:
  - saturated, changing fields instead of cream;
  - sentence-case serif with no italic flourishes or tracked-mono kickers;
  - the day structure and sun ring are specific to this restaurant's two
    rooms.
- **The ring is CSS, not the logo's brush stroke.** A vector of the real brush
  ring from the client would make the signature truly theirs.
- **Colour crossfades on long pages** need care on low-end phones. They are
  opacity-only, and reduced motion uses static grounds.

## 5. Option A: "The Blue Band" (assigned by the roll, corrected)

`sketch-a-*.png`

- **Thesis:** the Kampala commuter taxi's livery as the page's system. The
  white body is the ground, the **blue band** is the structural spine and
  reservation bar, the windows are photo apertures, the route boards are the
  two stops, and the windscreen visor strip is the navigation. Lugogo and
  Arena Mall are "two stops".
- **Palette:** Enamel `#F6F7F4`, Band blue `#1546C8` (white text 7.7:1), Tint
  `#0E1110`, Stop yellow `#F2C230` for the NEW STOP sticker, and logo gold on
  Tint only.
- **Type:** Big Shoulders Display (condensed signage grotesque) with Hanken
  Grotesk.
- **First viewport:** a dark visor nav; huge caps "WHERE WE ALL / COME
  TOGETHER."; three windows (Lugogo above Lugogo's board, Arena canopy above
  Arena's, cappuccino with a sliding-window mullion); the blue band with two
  route boards (`tel:`), Lugogo first with a NEW STOP sticker. On phones the
  band becomes a sticky bottom bar.
- **Signature:** a pinned horizontal "ride" past both restaurants' windows
  while the route board changes stop.
- **Fixes applied after review:**
  - windows now sit above their matching boards;
  - the banner is cropped out;
  - the mullion moved off the subject;
  - the kicker was removed.
- **Honest risks:**
  - The overall feel is streetwear and transit rather than "sophisticated".
  - It dilutes the restaurant's gold, serif and green identity.
  - The taxi reference is invisible to judges outside Kampala, and can carry
    "cheap and crowded" connotations locally.
  - The horizontal ride is a desktop-only, scroll-hijacking trope.
  - The exact band pattern is unverified: sources say "blue band/stripe", and
    a reviewer recalled a checkered band.

- **Reuse** `src/lib/motion` (`splitReveal`, `clipReveal`, `scrubMedia`,

  lives in a typed `src/content/home.ts`.
- **Fonts:** `next/font/google` (Gloock and Hanken Grotesk, self-hosted at
  build), replacing Geist.
- **Tokens:** colour, radius and type scale as CSS variables via Tailwind 4
  `@theme`.
- **Accessibility:**
  - each call stop is a single `tel:` link with an accessible name ("Call
    Patio Bella Lugogo Mall to reserve, 0794 299 074");
  - a skip link;
  - the sticky bar never covers focused elements (scroll padding);
  - AA contrast pairs only, as listed.
- **Motion lab:** keep `/motion-lab` as the regression page until the homepage
  ships.
